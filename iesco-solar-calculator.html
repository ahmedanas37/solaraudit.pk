IESCO Solar Net-Metering & 2026 Tariff Calculations (Islamabad & Rawalpindi)

Islamabad and Rawalpindi represent Pakistan's highest per-capita residential solar adoption zone. With large 10-Marla and 1-Kanal residences in CDA Sectors (F-6, F-7, F-8, F-10, G-11, G-13), DHA, and Bahria Town, homeowners face severe summer electricity bills driven by continuous multiple inverter AC usage. Under active 2026 IESCO tariff regulations, un-protected residential consumption exceeding 700 units reaches Rs. 48.84 per unit base tariff, excluding fuel price adjustments (FPA) and 18% GST.

Margalla Hills Climate & Lightning Protection Requirements

Unlike southern plains, the Islamabad/Rawalpindi region experiences intense monsoon lightning storms along the Margalla foothills and frequent winter temperature drops. When installing solar under IESCO jurisdiction:

  • Mandatory Surge Protection (SPD): Ensure your contractor installs dedicated DC Type-II SPDs on every MPPT string and an AC SPD at the distribution board.
  • Certified Copper Earth Pit: Soil resistivity testing must confirm an earthing resistance of < 5 Ohms, using pure copper earthing rods rather than cheap GI pipes.
  • Wind Load Engineering: Roof installations must withstand 120 km/h wind gusts typical of Islamabad pre-monsoon squalls.

IESCO Net-Metering NOC & Green Meter Timeline

IESCO sub-division offices typically process net-metering dossiers in 4 to 8 weeks. Your Sanctioned Load must match or exceed your proposed inverter capacity (e.g. minimum 10kW sanctioned load for a 10kW three-phase inverter).
