Here is why that math is physically and financially flawed under NEPRA's active 2026 regulatory framework, and what the true numbers look like.

---

### 1. The Myth: "1 Unit Exported = 1 Unit Imported"
Many salesmen tell homeowners: *"Bhai, if you generate 1,000 units and consume 1,000 units, your bill will be zero."*

**The Reality in 2026:**
- **Retail Import Tariffs** (nighttime or when clouds pass) are progressive: upper residential slabs exceed **Rs. 50 – 68 / kWh** (plus GST, FPA, and electricity duty).
- **Net-Billing Export Credit** is tied to NAEPP (National Average Energy Purchase Price), which floats around **Rs. 21.00 – 22.50 / kWh**.
- That means **1 unit exported during peak daylight only offsets ~0.35 units imported at night!**
- If your contractor sized your system purely to match total gross units without accounting for daytime self-consumption vs nighttime import, your residual monthly bill will NOT be zero. You will still receive a bill of Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 25,000 depending on your DISCO.

---

### 2. The Real Payback Horizon (Honest Engineering Physics)
When you account for:
1. **Realistic Irradiance Derating**: In Pakistan's harsh urban climates (Karachi humidity/haze, Lahore summer smog and dust), 1kW DC yields **~3.8 to 4.2 usable units/day** (a realistic 0.78 derating factor), NOT the 5.0+ units optimistic brochures promise.
2. **Daytime Self-Consumption**: The real goldmine of solar is direct daytime consumption. Running your inverter ACs, refrigerator, and fans directly on solar displaces the **Rs. 60+ progressive retail slabs**.
3. **Real Net Payback**:
   - An on-grid 5kW to 10kW system actually pays for itself in **2.2 to 2.8 years** (still an unbelievable ~40% tax-free annual ROI).
   - Anyone telling you 12 to 18 months without massive industrial day loads is giving you fabricated numbers.

---

### 3. The 4 Red Flags to Check on Any Contractor Quote
Before handing over any token advance, demand these 4 items in writing:
1. **Tier-1 Serial Authenticity**: Demand original laser-etched barcodes matching manufacturer flash-test databases (Longi Hi-MO X6, Jinko Tiger Neo, JA Solar). Many vendors import re-labeled Grade-B or defective reject modules from China and sell them as Tier-1.
2. **Pure Tinned Copper DC Wire (Min 6mm²)**: Refuse CCA (Copper-Clad Aluminum) or undersized 4mm² wire. In summer roof temperatures exceeding 55°C, CCA degrades rapidly and is a severe fire hazard.
3. **Dedicated Earth Boring (< 5 Ohms)**: Do not let electricians tie your inverter earth to your house plumbing or main distribution neutral. It must have its own dedicated copper rod boring with verified ground resistance.
4. **Net-Metering Regulatory SLA**: Stipulate a strict contractual deadline (e.g., 45 days) for the contractor to secure the DISCO green meter NOC.
