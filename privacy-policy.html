Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. Commitment to User Privacy

At SOLARAUDIT.ONLINE, accessible at https://solaraudit.online, your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is received and collected by SOLARAUDIT.ONLINE and how it is used.

2. Client-Side Computational Privacy

Unlike conventional solar lead generation websites, SOLARAUDIT.ONLINE does not collect or transmit your personal calculations to any remote server.

  • All inputs (monthly electricity bill amounts, appliance counts, selected city, DISCO, and contractor quotations) are processed purely within your device's web browser using client-side JavaScript.
  • We do not request or store your full name, phone number, CNIC, residential address, or utility account reference number.
  • Engineering PDF generation occurs entirely in-memory using client-side JavaScript (jsPDF) and is downloaded directly to your local file system.

3. Log Files & Web Analytics

Like many standard websites, SOLARAUDIT.ONLINE makes use of standard hosting server log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the site, track user's movement around the site, and gather broad demographic information. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons (Google AdSense & Partners)

SOLARAUDIT.ONLINE may display third-party advertisements served by Google AdSense.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

To ensure optimal performance and security, our application utilizes public Content Delivery Networks (including Tailwind CSS CDN and Cloudflare cdnjs for jsPDF). These CDNs may collect technical data (such as user agent and IP address) in accordance with their respective security and privacy policies.

6. Consent & Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated modification timestamp.

For inquiries regarding this privacy policy or our engineering methodology, please contact: contact@solaraudit.online.
