Solar Battery Guide Pakistan: Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) vs Tubular

The single most critical financial decision when purchasing a hybrid solar system in Pakistan is battery chemistry selection. Contractors frequently push deep-cycle lead-acid tubular batteries due to lower upfront shelf prices, but fail to disclose that tubular batteries are fundamentally unsuited for heavy night AC loads.

Engineering Comparison: LiFePO4 Lithium vs Lead-Acid Tubular

Parameter Lead-Acid Tubular (Phoenix/Osaka) LiFePO4 Lithium (Narada/Pylontech/Dyness)
Usable Depth of Discharge (DoD) 50% maximum (discharging lower ruins plates) 85% – 90% usable capacity
Cycle Life (Lifespan) 600 to 900 cycles (12 to 24 months on AC load) 4,000 to 6,000 cycles (10 to 15 years)
Inverter AC Inductive Load Severe voltage sag; causes inverter fault codes Smooth, stable 48V-51V DC output with smart BMS
Maintenance & Safety Emits toxic acid fumes; requires monthly distilled water Zero fumes, zero maintenance, compact wall-mount
10-Year True Cost of Ownership High (Requires 5 full battery replacements) Lowest (Single upfront investment)

How to Size a Battery Bank for Running an AC Overnight

A modern 1.5-ton DC inverter AC running at 26°C consumes an average of 600 to 750 Watts per hour. Over an 8-hour summer night, total energy consumption is approximately 5.0 to 6.0 kilowatt-hours (kWh).

To safely supply 5.5 kWh without stressing the cells:

  • Recommended Lithium Setup: 1x 48V 100Ah or 48V 150Ah LiFePO4 wall-mount module (5.1 kWh to 7.2 kWh capacity) costing approximately Rs. 2.1L to Rs. 2.8 Lakhs.
  • Tubular Alternative (Not Recommended): Would require 4x massive 250Ah 12V tubular batteries connected in series (Rs. 2.4L to Rs. 2.8L), which will rapidly deteriorate under inductive compressor starting loads within 14 months.
